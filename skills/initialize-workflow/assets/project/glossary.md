Compare proposed terms and changes in meaning with existing entries and relevant project usage. If a name has conflicting meanings, resembles another name, or may describe the same concept under a different name, ask the user to review the terms and where they appear. Ask whether to keep the terms distinct, choose a canonical term and record aliases, or rename a term. Track open questions under **Naming decisions to review** and hold dependent changes until the user decides. After review, update the entries and affected references in the applicable workflow stage.
